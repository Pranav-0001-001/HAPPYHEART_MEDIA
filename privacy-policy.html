Privacy Policy

[DRAFT FOR REVIEW] How client information is collected, used, and protected when you work with HAPPY HEART MEDIA.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information Collected

When you submit a project brief, create a client account, or contact me through forms on this website, I may collect:

  • Personal Contact Information: Your full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and company name.
  • Project Brief Details: Service requirements, project goals, timeline preferences, budget brackets, and reference links (e.g. Figma, Google Drive).
  • Account Credentials: Securely hashed passwords for authentication in the Client Portal (never stored in plain text).

2. How Your Information Is Used

The collected information is used solely to:

  • Review and respond to your project briefs with accurate quotes and roadmaps.
  • Communicate regarding ongoing development, design iterations, and campaign milestones.
  • Allow you to track project status securely in your Client Portal dashboard.

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Project submissions and account data are securely stored using cloud database encryption and industry-standard security practices. Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt before storage.

4. Data Deletion & Access Requests

You have the right to request a copy of your stored data or ask for the permanent deletion of your project briefs and account at any time.

To request data deletion or update your records, please email:
contact@happyheartmedia.com

5. Third-Party Integrations

If you choose to initiate communication via external platforms such as WhatsApp or Instagram, your interaction is also governed by the respective privacy policies of Meta Platforms, Inc.
